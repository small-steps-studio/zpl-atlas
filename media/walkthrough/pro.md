# Pro reports

* **Show Barcode Check**: Every barcode with its type, position and data, and a check of its check digit or format.
* **Show Layout Check**: The label size in millimetres at 203 and 300 dpi, and the fields that fall outside the print area.

The first Pro command starts a free 14-day trial on this computer. After the trial, Pro is a one-time purchase: USD 19 (Personal) or USD 39 per user (Business).
